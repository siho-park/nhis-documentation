# Program and data

Understanding the data starts with the program that generates it. Every record in the NHIS databases is a by-product of enrolling people, collecting premiums, paying claims, or running the national health screening program, and each of these processes shapes what the data can and cannot measure.

- [The health insurance system](system.md) describes who is covered, how benefits and cost sharing work, and how the NHIS differs from the Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service (HIRA).
- [NHIS research databases](databases.md) lists the databases available to researchers.
- [Applying for data](access.md) describes the application and its review.
- [Using the data](using.md) describes where the data are analyzed, the analysis centers, and how results leave the analysis environment.
