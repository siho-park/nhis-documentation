# The health insurance system

Korea has a single public insurer, the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS), which covers almost the entire population. Coverage became universal in 1989, and the many separate insurance societies were merged into a single insurer in 2000. Because every resident is enrolled with the same insurer, the NHIS data follow people across jobs, regions, and providers without the gaps that arise when people switch insurers.

## Who is covered

The population falls into three groups, and the group a person belongs to determines how their premium is set and how they appear in the data.

- **Employee insured (직장가입자)** are wage workers and their employers, who pay premiums based on wages.
- **Self-employed insured (지역가입자)** are everyone else not covered as a dependent, who pay premiums based on income and property.
- **Dependents (피부양자)** are family members of employee insured who meet income and property conditions, and who pay no premium of their own.

Low-income households are covered by Medical Aid (의료급여) rather than health insurance. Medical Aid is financed by the government and has its own cost-sharing rules, but its beneficiaries also appear in the NHIS eligibility data.

## Benefits and cost sharing

Services are divided into covered services (급여), which are billed to the insurer with patient cost sharing, and non-covered services (비급여), which patients pay for entirely out of pocket. Only covered services are billed to the insurer, so non-covered services do not appear in the claims data. This matters for any measure of total spending, since the share of non-covered services varies across conditions, provider types, and years.

## The NHIS and HIRA

Two public agencies handle claims, and both release research data. Providers submit claims to the Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service (HIRA, 건강보험심사평가원), which reviews them and passes the decisions to the NHIS for payment. The two agencies therefore hold overlapping claims records, but only the NHIS holds enrollment, premium, health screening, and linked death records.

| | NHIS | HIRA |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Insurer, collects premiums and pays providers | Reviews claims and assesses quality of care |
| Enrollment and premium data | Yes | No |
| Health screening results | Yes | No |
| Death records | Linked from Statistics Korea | No |
| Claims | Yes | Yes |

## Health screening

The NHIS runs the national health screening program (국가건강검진), and screening results are available in several research databases. Most adults are invited for a general health screening every two years, while non-office workers are screened every year, and separate programs cover cancer screening and infants and children.

## Long-term care

The NHIS also administers long-term care insurance for older adults (노인장기요양보험), introduced in July 2008.
