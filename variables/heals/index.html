    National Health Screening Cohort (건강검진코호트DB)¶

    This page lists all 458 variables of the National Health Screening Cohort in 11 tables, as described in the NHIS documentation listed below. Select a variable to see its Korean and English names, its description, and the meaning of each code.

    Background on sampling, versions, and linkage is on the dataset page.
