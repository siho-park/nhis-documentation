    National Sample Cohort (표본코호트DB)¶

    This page lists all 369 variables of the National Sample Cohort in 12 tables, as described in the NHIS documentation listed below. Select a variable to see its Korean and English names, its description, and the meaning of each code.

    Background on sampling, versions, and linkage is on the dataset page.

    • NSC layout, Ver 1.5, 2026-05 (표본코호트DB 레이아웃.xlsx)
